Why families choose us

One team, one quote, no surprises on the day

Decoration rarely goes wrong on taste. It goes wrong when a setup runs late, a price changes at the end, or nobody picks up the phone. We built the way we work around those three things.

  • An itemised written quote. You see what each part costs before deciding — not one lump sum.
  • A limited number of events per day. We'd rather decline a date than spread the team thin across yours.
  • Setup and cleanup both included. We arrive early and take everything away afterwards.
  • One point of contact. The same person from first message to setup day.
Before you enquire

Straight answers to the usual questions

How much does decoration cost?

It depends on the occasion, guest count, theme complexity and venue — a home backdrop and a full banquet-hall setup are very different jobs. Tell us what you're planning and you'll get an itemised written quote, free, with no obligation to continue.

Does enquiring commit me to anything?

No. The form starts a conversation. Nothing is held or booked until you've seen a written quote, approved it, and paid the advance. There's no charge for asking.

Which areas of Bangalore do you cover?

All of Bengaluru — including Koramangala, HSR Layout, Indiranagar, Whitefield, Jayanagar, JP Nagar, Marathahalli and Electronic City. Tell us your venue and we'll confirm straight away.

How far in advance should I book?

About a week is comfortable for most setups, and we've managed shorter notice plenty of times. Weekends and festival dates fill first, and because we cap how many events we take per day, popular dates do genuinely run out.

Can you match a specific theme or reference photo?

Yes — send us the picture. We'll tell you honestly what we can recreate, what we'd adapt for your space, and what the difference costs, before you pay anything.

What if I need to change or cancel?

Talk to us as early as you can. We'll move you to another available date wherever possible. If we can't make a new date work, we'll tell you honestly rather than leave you waiting.
