Why families choose us

One team, one quote, no surprises on the day

Most decoration problems aren't about taste — they're about a setup running late, a price changing at the end, or nobody picking up the phone. We built the way we work around fixing exactly those three things.

  • An itemised written quote. You see what each part costs before you decide, not a single lump-sum number.
  • A limited number of events per day. We'd rather turn a date down than spread the team thin across your celebration.
  • Setup and cleanup both included. We arrive early, and we take everything away afterwards.
  • Decoration plus whatever else you need. Photography, catering or entertainment can go into the same quote.

Before you enquire

Straight answers to the usual questions

Do you show prices before I enquire?

Yes — every setup in our catalogue shows its price. Your final quote depends on guest count, theme complexity, add-ons and venue, and it always comes to you in writing before any payment.

Does filling in the form confirm a booking?

No. The form just starts a conversation. Nothing is held or booked until you've seen a written quote, approved it, and paid the advance. There's no charge for enquiring and no obligation to continue.

Which areas of Bangalore do you cover?

All of Bengaluru — including Koramangala, HSR Layout, Indiranagar, Whitefield, Jayanagar, JP Nagar, Marathahalli and Electronic City. Tell us your venue and we'll confirm straight away.

How far in advance should I book?

About a week is comfortable for most setups, and we've managed shorter notice plenty of times. Weekends and festival dates fill first, and because we cap how many events we take per day, popular dates genuinely do run out.

Can I combine decoration with photography or catering?

Yes. Tell us what else you need and we'll coordinate it into a single quote rather than sending you off to find separate vendors and manage them yourself on the day.

What happens if I need to change the date?

Talk to us as early as you can and we'll move you to another available date wherever possible. If we can't make a new date work, we'll tell you honestly rather than leave you waiting.
