Sparkling Greece Arch Decor

A pale blue and white setting built from open metal frames rather than a solid backdrop — square gate frames and two tall arched panels, with a crystal chandelier hung in the central arch. Soft blue hydrangea and white roses climb the frames with trailing greenery, and white sheer curtains fall behind so light passes through the whole structure. A curved modern chaise in white sits at the centre, with candle holders and a low floral cluster on the floor.
