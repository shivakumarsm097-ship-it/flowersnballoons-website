Glorious Moana Theme Decor

Island-adventure-led decor on a themed backdrop setup. Our most-booked scale, and the point where the decor really starts to carry the room. Suits kids’ parties at home, in a clubhouse or a party hall. Colours and scale can be adjusted to your venue — tell us the space and we will size it properly.

What's included

  • A fabric or flex backdrop
  • An organic balloon garland (~150 balloons)
  • Themed cut-outs and props for the island adventure look
  • Focus lighting on the backdrop
  • A dressed cake table
  • Photo-booth props
  • Delivery, setup and cleanup

Indicative for a setup at this size — quantities and exact items are confirmed on your written quote, sized to your venue.

Travel & setup

Free within 12 km of our base in Bengaluru. Beyond that, travel is quoted before you pay — never added afterwards. Setup takes 2–4 hours depending on size; we arrive well before your guests.

Changes & cancellation

Swap colours or add items any time before setup day and we'll re-quote openly. Cancel more than 5 days ahead for a full refund of your advance; closer than that we'll do our best, since materials are usually already bought.

How payment works

A small advance confirms your date. Balance is due on the event day. We check the date is genuinely free before taking any money.
